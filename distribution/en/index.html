Seven Robbers

7 Qaraqshy · 7 қарақшы · Zheti Qaraqshy

Seven criminals. One nuclear detonator. 1978.

1978. Dinmukhamed Kunayev assembles seven professional criminals and gives them a dangerous secret mission: steal a nuclear detonator and prevent the largest explosion at the Semipalatinsk test site. Using all their criminal skills, the “7 Qaraqshy” are drawn into a mission on which the fate of an entire nation depends.

The makers call it the first Kazakh “istern”, an eastern-western: western, heist, drama and comedy. The crew built a bunker set, a rocket, and one car out of two UAZ vehicles. German actor Vincenz Kiefer joins the cast.

Shymyr

Шымыр

He only came for a tractor part.

Shymyr drives into town to pick up a tractor part and stays with his army friend Yeldos. The trip turns into a clash with a powerful criminal group. To people used to settling everything with force, money and connections, he answers with what he does best.

A home-grown action film with a distinctly Kazakh spirit: original fight choreography and stunts by Nomad Stunts.

Tokyldaq

Тоқылдақ · Токылдак

Whatever he draws comes true.

Olzhas wears a neck brace and draws in a notebook given to him by a baksy, a folk shaman. Soon he notices that what he draws comes true. The school, his home and the night corridors turn into a place where drawings become real.

The film’s theme is teenage bullying, which here takes on a frightening, supernatural form. Original language: Kazakh and Russian.
